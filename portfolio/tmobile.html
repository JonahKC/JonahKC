T-Mobile Club

T-Mobile Club began as a group of my friends getting T-Mobile Tuesday rewards together every week. I thought it would be silly to create a formal T-Mobile club, but soon after decided to see how far I could take it. We quickly became the largest club at Lincoln High School and built relationships with T-Mobile and its parent company, Deutsche Telekom. Deutsche Telekom was especially enthusiastic about our club, and we produced content for their social media and for a conference at their headquarters in Germany. We also hosted T-Mobile guest speakers and toured their headquarters in Bellevue.
